### Cost Center

- Cost Center spend, its daily trend and component breakdown, the month-end projection, budget alerts, and the 90% budget check that pauses automatic pipeline runs now all count costs by UTC usage date for the current UTC calendar month. Previously a server whose clock was not in UTC could record today's costs under a different date than the summary read, so spend could briefly disappear from, or be counted in the wrong month of, the summary around midnight and month boundaries. Costs dated in the next month no longer count toward this month's total.
